Course Overview : Basics

"For a Muslim, the Arabic language isn't just a skill it is the very breath of our faith."

To truly hold the Qur'an in your heart, you must first be able to read and write its sacred letters with clarity and soul. Our Arabic Alphabet Course is designed to build that unbreakable foundation, blending the beauty of Khat Naskh calligraphy with precise Makharij training to ensure every letter is articulated from its rightful place. We guide you through a transformative journey, teaching you to read letter by letter, then word by word, until full sentences flow with ease. As the essential Gateway to the Nuzul Program, this course recognizes that a strong base in reading and writing is everything. Don't just look at the verses learn to write them, speak them, and live them. Welcome to the Gateway of the Nuzul Program. Take your first step and build your base today.

Goal

To build a strong, confident foundation that prepares students to enter the Nuzul Program with clarity and readiness.

Focus

Mastering Arabic letters through structured reading, writing, and Makharij training.

The Experience

Step-by-step interactive live sessions where students move from letters to flowing sentences combining calligraphy practice with precise pronunciation and personal guidance
